Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Midnight Stories and its publishing tool, Midnight Stories Publisher ("the app"), handle information.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Midnight Stories channel to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not available to the public.

Information the app accesses

  • TikTok: when the channel owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information of that account (open ID, display name, avatar) through the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. This is used only to confirm the correct account is connected and to upload the channel's videos.
  • Instagram: the app uses the Instagram API to publish the channel's Reels and to read comments on the channel's own posts in order to count audience votes (for example "closet" or "blanket"). Only the vote keyword counts are kept; usernames and comment text are not stored.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers, does not use tracking cookies, and does not post on behalf of anyone other than the Midnight Stories accounts.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's private hosting environment and are only sent to the official TikTok and Meta API endpoints. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok and Meta account settings.

Data deletion

Disconnecting the app in TikTok or Meta settings revokes its access. Any stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @midnightstories2255 on Instagram.
