MIDNIGHT STORIES

Midnight Stories is a faceless channel of short animated horror stories told in the second person. Every series runs for 6–7 parts, and every part ends with a choice. The audience votes in the comments, and the next part follows the majority.

Where to watch

  • Instagram: @midnightstories2255
  • TikTok: @midnight.stories2255

Midnight Stories Publisher

Midnight Stories Publisher is a private tool used only by the Midnight Stories creator to publish the channel's own videos to its own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It:

  • lets the channel owner sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to authorise the channel account;
  • uploads finished episodes that the owner has reviewed and approved, with their caption and the AI-generated content label;
  • reads basic profile information of the authorised account to confirm it is the correct channel.

The tool is not offered to other users, does not post on anyone else's behalf, and does not collect data about viewers.
