# The 3:17 Knock (7 parts)

**Hook:** You live alone on the 4th floor. Every night at 3:17 someone knocks. Nobody is ever there.

**Rules of the entity**
- It can only act at 3:17 and for exactly 17 minutes after.
- It copies voices it has heard inside the apartment — including yours.
- It cannot cross a threshold that is lit.

**Secret (revealed part 6–7):** The thing in the bed is the previous tenant, who has been "replaced" by the knocker; the closet voice is the real you from a night you don't remember.

**Arc**
1. Knock moves from the front door to the closet; something is in your bed. (A: open closet / B: pull off blanket)
2. Consequence of choice; learn the 17-minute rule.
3. The neighbour on floor 3 has the same knock.
4. Twist: the knocks began the night you moved in — the landlord knew.
5. Climax: survive until 03:34.
6. Reveal of who is in the bed / closet.
7. Ending + hint that it moved to another floor.
