# The Last Train (7 parts)
**Hook:** You fall asleep on the last train and wake up in a full carriage of passengers who don't blink. It stops at a station that isn't on the map.
**HUD:** STATION ??? · clock 01:13 · NEXT STOP IN → DOORS CLOSE IN 0:30
**Rules of the line:** passengers never blink; they only move when the doors are open; each stop takes something from you (a memory, a name, your reflection).
**Arc:** 1 wake up / first stop · 2 consequence · 3 a passenger who looks like you · 4 the conductor · 5 the map with your name on it · 6 the last station · 7 ending: you're a passenger now – or you drive the train.
**Choices are always:** get off / stay on (+ a twist variant per part).
